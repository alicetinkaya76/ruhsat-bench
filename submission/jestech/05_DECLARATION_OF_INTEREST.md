# Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies

This declaration separates three distinct uses, because they carry different
weight and conflating them would understate one of them.

**1. AI systems as the object of study.** The paper evaluates commercial and
open-weight language models. This is the subject matter of the research and is
described in full in §3 and §4.

**2. A language-model adjudication pass on seventeen borderline gold labels.**
This is a methodological use and is disclosed in §4.6.5 of the manuscript,
together with the decision not to adopt the three label changes it proposed and
the pre-registration grounds for that decision. Those seventeen claims remain
flagged `needs_human_review`.

**3. Use in preparing this work.** The author used a generative AI assistant
(Anthropic Claude) in the preparation of this manuscript. Its use was not
limited to language polishing: it was used to write and run the analysis and
harness code, to compute the reported statistics from the committed run records,
to draft and revise the manuscript text, and to conduct adversarial internal
review rounds against the pre-registration. The scientific decisions — the
research question, the study design, the pre-registrations and their annexes,
the choice of target journal, and the decision not to adopt the label changes
described above — were made by the author. The blinded gold-label audit was
carried out by two human domain specialists.

The author reviewed and edited the output, verified every reported figure against
the committed number sheet released with the archive, and takes full
responsibility for the content of this publication. The assistant is not, and
under Elsevier policy could not be, an author.
