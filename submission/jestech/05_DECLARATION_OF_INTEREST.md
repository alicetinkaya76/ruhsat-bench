# Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies

Three distinct uses arise in this work and are separated here, because they carry
different weight and merging them would understate one of them.

**1. AI systems as the object of study.** This paper evaluates commercial and
open-weight language models. That is the subject matter of the research and is
described in full in Sections 3 and 4.

**2. A language-model adjudication pass on seventeen borderline gold labels.**
This is a methodological use and is disclosed in Section 4.6.5 of the manuscript,
together with the decision *not* to adopt the three label changes it proposed and
the pre-registration grounds for that decision. Those seventeen claims remain
flagged `needs_human_review`; no gold label used in this paper was set by a
language model.

**3. Use in preparing this work.** During the preparation of this work the author
used Anthropic's Claude in order to write and run the analysis and harness code,
to compute the reported statistics from the committed run records, to draft and
revise the manuscript text, and to conduct adversarial internal review rounds
against the pre-registration. This use went beyond language polishing and is
declared as such rather than under the narrower formula.

The scientific decisions were the author's: the research question, the study
design, the pre-registration and its eight annexes, the choice of target journal,
and the decision to reject the proposed label changes described above. The
blinded gold-label audit was performed by two human specialists. Several errors
introduced during AI-assisted drafting — including two incorrect publication
years and an unsupported claim about the magnitude of one result — were caught in
the internal review rounds and are corrected in this version; the review record
and the number sheet documenting every reported figure are released with the
archive.

After using this tool, the author reviewed and edited the content as needed and
takes full responsibility for the content of the publication. The tool is not,
and under the journal's policy could not be, an author.
